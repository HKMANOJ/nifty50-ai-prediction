What this replay can—and cannot—show

It shows the logged main-scanner decisions at the selected time. Qualified rows outside the retained payload stay qualified; missing rows are unobserved, not rejected. Completion-marker and row-count checks assess log consistency—not market-price accuracy.

The BO / BD time is a historical candle label, not when the stock entered Top-5. Daily pinned picks, “Top 5 Eligible”, browser visibility and historical candle charts cannot be reconstructed exactly from ranking rows alone. This view does not fetch today's candles, simulate trades or measure option profit.
